# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences land here: potential clients (brands and companies scouting a designer to hire or commission), and recruiters or employers evaluating Marianna "Mari" Ventura for a design role. Both arrive comparing her against other designers' portfolios and are trying to answer the same question quickly — is this someone worth reaching out to.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site for a Brazilian graphic designer, showcasing visual identity and branding work. It exists to get her hired — as a freelance commission or a job — by making her skill and credibility legible fast. Success is a visitor reaching out through Contato.

## Positioning

Bold, distinctive visual identity work — not safe, not generic. The claim every page reinforces: this designer takes real creative risks and pulls them off.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: contact directly via Contato (email/LinkedIn) is primary; browsing the Trabalhos portfolio is the secondary, lower-commitment path for visitors not ready to reach out.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Identidade visual ousada, feita com cuidado." (Bold visual identity, made with care.)
- Belief ladder: (1) the portfolio grid proves the work looks distinctive and skilled, (2) Sobre proves real experience and credibility, (3) Contato makes reaching out feel easy and low-risk.
- Proof on hand: one populated case study (Festival Afrontosas) and the work history in Sobre are live in the code now. More projects are coming — Mari has several additional pieces to add to Trabalhos beyond what's currently there.

## Brand Personality

Playful and expressive, but composed by an editorial hand — personality-forward without tipping into chaos. The existing deep-purple identity with a light-blue accent, paired with Fraunces for display type and Inter for body, already carries this: confident serif headlines against a clean, restrained sans for everything functional.

## Anti-references

Should not read as an overly corporate or stiff resume site. This is a designer's site, not a LinkedIn profile — it should feel authored, not templated into professional neutrality.

## Design Principles

- Bold but considered: distinctive, risk-taking work presented with editorial polish, never corporate stiffness.
- Show the work first: visuals lead every page; credentials support, they don't open.
- Build trust in sequence: portfolio proves skill, Sobre proves credibility, Contato removes friction — don't ask for the reach-out before the proof.
- Expressive within a system: playfulness lives in motion, color, and type choices constrained by one consistent identity, not scattered improvisation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific accessibility requirements beyond standard good practice; visual impact takes priority over accommodating unusual constraints.
